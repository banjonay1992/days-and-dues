For your work

There’s more than one way
to work for yourself.

Different work. Familiar admin. Days & Dues is for the clients, hours, costs and invoices that sit behind what you do.

01

Creative & digital

From the brief
to the final file.

Designers, photographers, writers and editors: keep each project with its client, record your time and costs, and turn completed work into an invoice.

ONE WAY TO USE IT

A brand project, two editing sessions and a travel receipt — together under one client.

02

Consulting & coaching

Good conversations.
Clearer records.

Plan sessions, keep useful work notes and record the time you spend preparing and meeting. An online appointment doesn’t need a work address.

ONE WAY TO USE IT

Plan a video consultation, log preparation time and create an invoice afterwards.

03

Teaching & tutoring

Keep the lesson.
Lose the loose ends.

Keep client details, plan your teaching time and record completed sessions. Find the hours you need when you sit down to invoice.

ONE WAY TO USE IT

Add a client, plan a lesson and record the session before the next one begins.

04

Beauty & wellbeing

Your clients.
Your working rhythm.

Plan appointments, keep work and contact details together, and record time and business expenses around your services.

GOOD TO KNOW

Designed for business admin. It isn’t a clinical record system or a public booking website.

05

Home & local services

Between visits,
stay on top of the details.

Gardeners, cleaners, pet carers and tradespeople: add the work location, keep notes or photos with a visit, and track costs and invoices.

ONE WAY TO USE IT

A garden visit, three hours of work and the supplies you bought along the way.

06

Your own mix

Doesn’t fit a box?
That’s fine.

Choose “Other self-employed work” during setup. Use the same client, work, time and invoice tools in the way that makes sense for you.
