All questions ↗
Is it for my kind of self-employed work?

If your day involves clients, work to keep track of, hours, costs or invoices, it can fit. Projects can happen online or in person, and a work address is optional. See some examples.

Will the trial charge me automatically?

No. The one-time 3-day app trial starts after eligible accounts finish setup. It does not start an Apple subscription. You choose whether to subscribe separately.

What happens to my existing account?

Your account, records and existing subscription continue with the update. Keep using the same login. The redesigned version is currently awaiting Apple review.
