Subscription help ↗

The small print, plainly

No guesswork.

Do I need to cancel the three-day app trial?

No. The app trial doesn’t create a paid subscription and won’t charge you. If you separately subscribe through Apple, manage that subscription in Apple’s settings.

Does my existing subscription still work?

Yes. The rebrand uses the same subscription product. Keep using the same app account; there is no need to buy it again.

Are invoices and tax estimates available worldwide?

Invoices currently use GBP. Tax estimates follow UK sole-trader rules. Check those limits against your needs before subscribing.

Can I restore a purchase?

Use Restore purchase on the subscription screen while signed in to the app account associated with your purchase and the Apple account used to pay. Get help if access is missing.
