The app

A home for
the whole working day.

Plan it, do it, remember it, invoice it. Days & Dues brings the everyday admin around your work into one iPhone app.

WorkClientsTime & notesInvoicesMoney

01 / Work

Every project starts
with a little space.

Keep an appointment, a one-off project or a piece of ongoing work alongside its client.

  • Plan a date and time, and keep track of progress.
  • Choose an existing client without typing their details again.
  • Add a location when it matters. Leave it blank for remote work.
  • Keep notes, time, costs and photos with the work.
From first setup to first invoice ↗

02 / Clients

Remember the details.
Make room for the people.

A client doesn’t need a project straight away. Save their details now, and add work when you’re ready.

  • Create clients directly from the Clients screen.
  • See their work together in one place.
  • Keep the contact details you need for invoices and follow-ups.
  • Reuse an existing client when new work comes in.

03 / Time & notes

Tell us about your day.
Then make it yours.

Speak or type a note while it’s fresh. AI helps organise it into suggested work entries, time and expenses. You review the details before they are saved.

Prefer to do it yourself? Start a timer or enter your time and costs manually. Voice and AI are optional, with an in-app consent step.

An example day note
“Two hours editing for Alex, and twelve pounds on the train.”

TIME2 hours

EXPENSE£12 · Travel

Check the client, work and amounts. You decide what to save.

04 / Invoices & quotes

Your work is done.
Keep the payment in view.

Create quotes and invoices, export a professional PDF and see what still needs to be paid.

  • Use your saved business and client details.
  • Review line items and amounts before sending.
  • Find unpaid and overdue invoices.
  • Use follow-up tools when an invoice needs a nudge.

Invoices currently use GBP. Sending an invoice does not guarantee payment.

05 / Money

A clearer view of
what comes in and goes out.

Bring invoice totals, recorded expenses and unpaid amounts into the same picture.

  • Keep costs and receipt images with your work.
  • See income and expenses in the Money screen.
  • Open invoices and quotes without hunting through tabs.
  • Use UK sole-trader tax estimates as a starting point to check.

Figures depend on your records. Tax estimates are not a tax return or personalised tax advice.
